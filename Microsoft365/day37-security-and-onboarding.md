# Day 37 - Security Fundamentals & Onboarding

## Key Concepts
Security is not just the security team's job; every L1 tech must spot phishing, manage MFA, and follow secure onboarding/offboarding (JML) procedures to prevent data breaches.

## Lab
Reviewed and practiced the security concepts from the HSBT L0 curriculum:
- Phishing Detection: Analyzing sender addresses and hovering over links.
- MFA: Resetting a user's MFA in Entra ID when they lose their phone.
- Onboarding: Creating an AD user, assigning an M365 license, and enforcing MFA on day one.

## Why It Matters
L1 is the first line of defense. Catching a phishing email or correctly provisioning access prevents massive security incidents.

## Interview Q&A
**Q: A user reports a suspicious email. What do you do?**
A: Tell them not to click it. Have them forward it to the security team and delete it. If they clicked, disconnect the PC from the network immediately.
