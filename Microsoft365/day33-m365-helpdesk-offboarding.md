# Day 33 - Helpdesk: Password Reset & Offboarding

## Key Concepts
Password resets are the most common L1 ticket. Offboarding requires blocking a user's sign-in rather than deleting their account to prevent data loss.

## Lab
- **Password Reset:** Selected "Test User 1", clicked "Reset password", and enforced the requirement for the user to change their password at next login.
- **Offboarding:** Navigated to the Account tab and checked "Block sign-in". This prevents the user from logging in while retaining their mailbox and OneDrive data.

## Why It Matters
If an employee leaves and you delete their account, you lose access to their emails and files. Blocking sign-in secures the account while allowing the company to transfer data to a manager.

## Interview Q&A
**Q: Why block a user's sign-in instead of deleting their account immediately?**
A: Deleting an account removes the mailbox and data. Blocking sign-in stops them from logging in immediately but keeps the data intact for transfer or compliance.
